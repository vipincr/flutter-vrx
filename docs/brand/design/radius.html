Radius

Corner radii in millimetres. A radius longer than half a control's short side draws a capsule, so pill is 100 mm and clamps.

Lengths are millimetres and type sizes are points in the standard. This page draws them at 160 ppi: 1 mm = 160 / 25.4 = 6.2992 px and 1 pt = 160 / 72 = 2.2222 px. Border widths are device pixels and stay pixels.
