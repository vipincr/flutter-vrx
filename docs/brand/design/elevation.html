Floors

Elevation

How far a surface stands off the one below it, and what each display can draw of that. The tonal step is a mix toward the text colour sized to the surface's lightness step; the standard names it in steps of CIE L*.

Lengths are millimetres and type sizes are points in the standard. This page draws them at 160 ppi: 1 mm = 160 / 25.4 = 6.2992 px and 1 pt = 160 / 72 = 2.2222 px. Border widths are device pixels and stay pixels.

Full

Colour, a drawn shadow for each elevation level, gradients as gradients.

Light

Colour, but a tonal step and an outline stand in for the shadow, and a gradient is its solid fallback.

Grey

Four greys from the grey map, an outline for each elevation level, a gradient is its grey fallback.

Epaper

Black and white from the 2-level map, an outline for each elevation level, a press inverts the control.
